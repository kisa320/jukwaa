Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

  • Show didn't happen: if a creator cancels or doesn't start a paid show within 30 minutes of the scheduled time, you get a full refund.
  • Show ended early: if a show ends in less than half its advertised time, you may request a partial refund.
  • Perks not delivered: paid perks (shout-outs, requests) not delivered during the show are refunded in full.
  • Tips are voluntary and non-refundable, except for proven unauthorised payments.
  • Creator registration fee (KES 50) is non-refundable once ID verification is completed.

How to request

Contact us within 7 days with your M-Pesa receipt number. Approved refunds are sent back to your M-Pesa number within 5 working days.
