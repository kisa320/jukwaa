Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We collect only what we need to run Jukwaa.

What we collect

  • Username, phone number and email you provide.
  • Payment records (amount, date, M-Pesa receipt number). We never receive or store your M-Pesa PIN or card details; those are handled by our payment partner.
  • Creator ID verification documents, used only to confirm age and identity.
  • Basic usage data (shows joined, ratings, reports) to calculate trust scores.

How we use it

To provide shows, process payments, prevent fraud, pay creators and comply with Kenyan law, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Sharing

We share data only with our payment partner and service providers needed to run the platform, or when required by law. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
